We describe here the discriminating variables used to separate
between the signal, the combinatorial background and the misreconstructed
$B$ decays.

The discriminating variables can be classified into three groups:
(a) $B$ meson variables, related to the reconstruction of the decay vertex and
to the collinearity between $\vec{p}^B$ and the separation between
production and decay vertices $\Delta\vec{x}$;
(b) variables describing the leptons and mesons forming the $B$ meson candidate;
and (c) variables related to the rest of the event.

The discriminating variables considered are the following:

\begin{itemize}
\item final states variable kinematic and isolation properties:
  \begin{itemize}
  \item $p_T$ of all final states particles: magnitude of the transverse momentum $\vec{p_T}$ ($4$ variables: two leptons and two mesons)
  \item pseudorapidity $\eta$ of all final states particles ($4$ variables: two leptons and two mesons)
  \item azimuthal angle $\phi$ of all final states particles ($4$ variables: two leptons and two mesons)
  \item isolation variable (``iso\_c40'') of all final states particles: definition to be included ($4$ variables: two leptons and two mesons)
  \item tracks dEdx ratio: definition to be included (how many variables? 1 or 4?)
  \end{itemize}
\item di-lepton and di-meson variables:
  \begin{itemize}
  \item di-lepton pvalue and di-meson pvalue: definition to be included ($2$ variables)
  \item di-lepton angle alpha sym and di-meson angle alpha sym: definition to be included ($2$ variables)
  \item di-lepton angle beta sym and di-meson angle beta sym: definition to be included ($2$ variables)
  \item d B di-lepton significance: definition to be included ($1$ variables)
  \item d B di-meson significance: definition to be included ($1$ variables)
  \item d diMeson di-lepton significance: definition to be included ($1$ variables)
  \item angle $\ell\ell$ plane mm plane: definition to be included ($1$ variables)
  \item di-meson $K\pi$ and $\pi K$ invariant masses: $m_{K\pi}$ and $m_{\pi K}$ where the first meson is the positive one,
    hence the $K\pi$ hypothesis tags a $\B0$ candidate and viceversa the $\pi K$ tags the $\Bb$ candidate ($2$ variables)
  \item dR trackPlus trackMinus: three-dimensional opening between the two mesons $\Delta R = \sqrt{\Delta\phi^2+\Delta\eta^2}$ (true?) ($1$ variables)
  \end{itemize}
\item $B$ candidate and vertex variables:
  \begin{itemize}
  \item $p_T$ of the $B$ candidate (four-track vertex) ($1$ variables)
  \item $\chi^2$ of the $B$ candidate (four-track vertex) ($1$ variables)
  \item a0 significance: definition to be included ($1$ variables)
  \item z0 significance: definition to be included ($1$ variables)
  \item $L_{xy}$ and its significance $L_{xy}/\sigma(L_{xy})$:
    Scalar product in the transverse plane of $\Delta \vec{x}\!\cdot\!\vec{p}^{B}/|\vec{p}^{B}|$ ($2$ variables)
  \item lifetime and lifetime significance: proper decay length $ct = L_{xy} \cdot m_B / p_T^B$ (divided by its uncertainty for the significance).
    The values of $m_B$ and $p_B$ are obtained from the refitted track momenta given by the vertex-fit for the fully reconstructed candidate.
    ($2$ variables)
  \item $\cos\theta$ and its significance ($|\alpha_{2\mathrm{D}}|$, pointing angle??):
    Angle in the transverse plane between the vector from the primary to the secondary
    vertex  $\Delta\vec{x}$ and the B candidate momentum $\vec{p}^B$ ($2$ variables)
  \item angle pp plane ee plane and angle pp plane mm plane: definition to be included ($2$ variables)
  \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
